Ilisus Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Policy is part of our Terms of Service. It applies to everyone who uses the Services, including Educators and Students. Violations may lead to removal of content, suspension or termination without refund, and, where appropriate, referral to law enforcement.

1. Protect the Content

You may not:

  1. copy, scrape, crawl, download in bulk, screen-record for redistribution, or archive any Experience, map, essay, image or dataset;
  2. share your login, sell or give away access, or post class links or codes publicly;
  3. bypass, disable or interfere with any paywall, license check, rate limit or other access control;
  4. reproduce Ilisus Content in another product, course, website, video or publication without written permission;
  5. use any Content or output of the Services to train, fine-tune, benchmark or prompt an AI model, or to build a dataset; or
  6. frame or mirror the Services, or remove Ilisus branding, attributions or license notices.

2. Protect the Services

You may not:

  1. probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the Services, except through a coordinated disclosure to hello@ilisus.com;
  2. introduce malware, overload the Services, or attempt denial-of-service attacks;
  3. access accounts, systems or data that are not yours; or
  4. reverse engineer or decompile the Services, except to the extent the law expressly permits.

3. Protect People

When you send us feedback, ideas or messages, you may not:

  1. include anyone else's personal information, especially a Student's;
  2. harass, threaten or defame anyone, or send hateful, sexually explicit or violent content;
  3. impersonate any person or misrepresent your affiliation with an Institution; or
  4. send spam, advertising or chain messages.

4. Use It Lawfully

You may not use the Services in violation of any law, including copyright, privacy, export control and sanctions laws, or in a way that would require Ilisus to obtain a license or registration it does not have.

Educators may display and discuss Experiences in class and share class links with their own enrolled Students as their License allows. Brief screenshots or short excerpts may be used in an Educator's own lesson materials for those Students, with credit to Ilisus, and may not be posted publicly or sold. 6. Reporting

Report violations or security issues to hello@ilisus.com. We investigate reports and may act without notice when needed to protect users or the Services.
