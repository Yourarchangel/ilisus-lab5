Ilisus Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: November 15, 2026

Ilisus wants every learner to be able to use our experiences. Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the standard the U.S. Department of Justice adopted for public schools and other state and local government websites under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act.

Where we are today

Our experiences are early releases built by a small team. Before Liberty launched, an automated accessibility check (axe) found no problems on its atlas, story pages and legal pages. Automated checks catch only part of what matters, and we have not yet completed a full manual review or a third-party audit, so we do not claim full conformance.

Known limitations

  • Some interactive map gestures do not yet have keyboard equivalents.
  • Some scanned historical documents are images of text; we are adding transcriptions.
  • Some animated map sequences may be hard to follow with a screen reader. The essay and event text tell the same story.

Feedback and alternate formats

If you have trouble using any part of Ilisus, email hello@ilisus.com with the subject line "Accessibility." Tell us the page and the problem. We will reply within 2 business days and, where we can, provide the content in another accessible format.

For schools

We will complete an accessibility conformance report (VPAT) on request and work with your district's accessibility team.

This statement describes our goals and current status. It does not create a warranty or contractual obligation beyond what our Terms of Service state.
