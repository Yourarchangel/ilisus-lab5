Ilisus Purchases, Billing and Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

This Policy is part of our Terms of Service. It explains how paid Licenses work, how long they last, and when you can get a refund.

1. Key Terms at a Glance

A LIBERTY LICENSE IS A ONE-TIME PAYMENT FOR LIFETIME ACCESS. IT DOES NOT RENEW OR EXPIRE, AND WE WILL NOT CHARGE YOU AGAIN FOR IT.

Item How it works
What you buy A License to one Experience, for one person, as described at checkout

2. Before and After You Buy

The checkout page shows the price, what the License includes, the access period, and that the payment is one-time. After purchase, we email a receipt that confirms the amount and the Experience.

3. No Renewal and No Expiration

Licenses bought for a one-time price do not renew and do not expire. If we ever offer a plan that renews automatically, we will update this Policy first, show the renewal terms clearly before you pay, ask for your consent to them, and let you cancel online at any time.

4. Free Previews and Promotion Codes

Free previews, such as Liberty's five-moment free preview, do not require payment information and never turn into a paid License on their own. We may give promotion codes to selected people. A code applies only to the product, amount and period stated with it, has no cash value, may not be sold or transferred, and may be withdrawn at any time before it is used. A License bought with a 100% discount code is a free License and does not qualify for a refund.

5. Refunds

5.1 14-day guarantee. If you are not satisfied, email hello@ilisus.com within 14 days of your purchase for a full refund. No reason is required. Your License ends when the refund is issued.

5.2 Other cases. After 14 days, fees are non-refundable, except where the law requires, where we remove an Experience as described in Section 13 of the Terms, or where we terminate for convenience under Section 15.3 of the Terms.

5.3 How refunds are paid. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days of approval, depending on your bank.

5.4 Abuse. We may decline repeated refund requests from the same person or Institution where we reasonably believe the guarantee is being misused.

6. Payments

6.1 Processor. Stripe processes payments and Outseta manages accounts and Licenses on our behalf. We never receive or store your full card number.

6.2 Chargebacks. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We will usually resolve it faster. We may suspend access while a chargeback is pending.

6.3 Taxes. Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes unless stated. We will collect tax where the law requires.

7. Schools and Institutions

7.1 Quotes and purchase orders. Institutions may request a quote at hello@ilisus.com. We accept purchase orders. Invoices are due within 30 days of the invoice date unless the Order says otherwise.

7.2 Tax-exempt purchases. Send a valid exemption certificate with your purchase order.

7.3 Vendor paperwork. We will provide a Form W-9 and complete reasonable vendor registration forms on request.

8. Transfers

Licenses are personal to the buyer and may not be resold or transferred, except that an Institution may reassign an Educator License to another Educator at the same Institution by emailing us.

9. Contact

Billing questions: hello@ilisus.com, with the subject line "Billing."
