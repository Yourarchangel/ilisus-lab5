Ilisus Educational Content, Accuracy and AI Disclosure

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Disclosure is part of our Terms of Service. It tells you how our experiences are made, what you can rely on, and what you should check.

1. How We Use AI

AI is a great enhancer. It lets a small team build in months what once took historians years. The humanities are still human first, so here is exactly how we use it:

  • Research and drafting. AI tools help us find sources, organize events, and draft essays and narration. A person reviews every essay against cited sources before release.
  • Maps and code. AI tools help write the software and process open geographic data. The underlying data comes from the sources on our Credits page.
  • Images. Where no historical image exists, we may use AI-generated or AI-assisted illustrations or reconstructions. Every one is labeled as AI-generated where it appears, and where possible we show the historical sources it was based on.
  • At play time. Our experiences run without AI. What you click, read or choose is not sent to any AI system.

We check AI output for factual errors and bias. We cannot guarantee we catch every one.

2. What Is Fact and What Is Interpretation

  • Essays state our interpretation of the historical record and cite the sources it rests on. Historians disagree on many questions, and we try to note real disagreements.
  • Quotations from historical figures and scholars are reproduced from the cited source. Spelling may be modernized.
  • Story experiences use narrative techniques. Any invented character, scene or dialogue is labeled, and the separate History view explains what the record supports.
  • Reconstructions of buildings, battles, routes and frontier lines are approximations drawn from historical maps and scholarship. Boundaries and positions may be uncertain.
  • Dates in ancient and scriptural material are shown as broad eras based on scholarship, not as settled facts.

3. Difficult History

We believe in objective truth and in letting every voice in the record speak, including voices that are uncomfortable to hear. Experiences depict war, slavery, the displacement of Native nations, religious conflict, disease and death as they happened. Period documents may use language that is offensive today. We include it to teach, not to endorse it. Where it helps, we add a content note before a scene.

4. Not Professional Advice; No Endorsement

Our experiences are educational. They are not legal, religious, medical, financial or professional advice, and they do not carry academic credit. Presenting a historical figure's words or a theologian's commentary does not mean Ilisus endorses that view.

5. Read Further

We want you asking questions. Every experience points to credible academic and primary sources. Please read them alongside our work, especially for anything you will teach, publish or rely on.

6. Corrections

If you find an error, a biased framing or a missing perspective, tell us at hello@ilisus.com or through the Give Ideas form on ilisus.com. We review every report. When we fix a substantive error we note the correction in the experience's version history.

7. No Warranty

We work hard to be accurate and fair. To the fullest extent permitted by law, all Content is provided "as is," and Ilisus does not warrant that it is complete, accurate, current or suitable for any purpose, as stated in Sections 17 and 18 of our Terms.
