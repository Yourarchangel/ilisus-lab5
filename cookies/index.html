Ilisus Cookie Notice

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Ilisus uses cookies and similar browser storage to run the Services, keep them secure, process payments and measure how our website is used. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or cross-site advertising trackers.

Name or type Set by Purpose Duration
ga, _ga[ID] Google Analytics Counts visits and measures which pages and experiences are used Up to 2 years
Outseta.nocode.accessToken (cookie on ilisus.com) and login session storage Outseta Keeps you signed in and checks your License, so a paid Experience opens fully Until you sign out or the session expires
__stripe_mid, __stripe_sid Stripe Fraud prevention during checkout Up to 1 year and 30 minutes
__cf_bm and similar Cloudflare Bot detection and security 30 minutes to 1 year
Local storage for experiences Ilisus Saves your place, settings and progress on your own device Until you clear your browser data

Analytics. Google Analytics runs through Google Tag Manager. We use it for measurement only, with Google signals and ad personalization turned off. Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts visits, without cookies.

Fonts. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a font loads.

Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout to stop Google Analytics. Blocking analytics cookies does not affect how the experiences work. If you block the Outseta, Stripe or Cloudflare items, sign-in, checkout or security checks may not work.
