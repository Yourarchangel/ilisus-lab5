Ilisus Copyright, DMCA and Trademark Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Ilisus respects the intellectual property of others and builds its experiences from public-domain, openly licensed, licensed and original materials. This Policy explains how to report a concern and how we respond, consistent with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. § 512 ("DMCA").

1. If You Believe Something Is Not Public Domain or Is Credited Wrongly

Most images and maps in our experiences are historical works we believe are in the public domain or are used under an open license, and each is listed on our Credits page. If you think we have made a mistake about a work's status or its credit, email hello@ilisus.com. We will review promptly and, where appropriate, correct the credit or remove the work. You do not need to send a formal DMCA notice for this.

2. Copyright Infringement Notices

If you believe material on the Services infringes a copyright you own or control, send a written notice to our designated agent (Section 5) that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed, or a representative list if several works are involved;
  3. identification of the material you claim is infringing and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it, such as the URL and a description;
  4. your name, mailing address, telephone number and email address;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and
  6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages. Consider whether fair use or the public domain applies before you send a notice.

3. Counter-Notices

If material you provided was removed and you believe it was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send our designated agent a counter-notice that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal;
  3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification; and
  4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district where your address is located (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district in which Ilisus may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice or their agent.

We will forward the counter-notice to the original complainant. If they do not notify us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action, we may restore the material within 10 to 14 business days after we received the counter-notice.

4. Repeat Infringers

We will terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the Accounts of users who are repeat infringers.
