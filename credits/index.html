Ilisus Credits and Licenses

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Ilisus experiences are built on the work of museums, libraries, archives, open-data projects and open-source developers. We are grateful to them. This page gives the credits and license notices those sources require. Each experience also has its own credits list, generated from our license records, linked at the bottom of this page.

A public-domain or openly licensed source being listed here does not mean that source endorses Ilisus.
