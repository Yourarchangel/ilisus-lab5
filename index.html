The name

A river, a tree, and a conversation.

The Ilisos was a small river just outside the walls of ancient Athens. In Plato's Phaedrus, Socrates walks its bank with a friend and sits down to talk in the shade of a plane tree.

Learning there happened outdoors, in conversation, at the pace of the people walking. We took the name because that is the kind of learning we want to build.

A fair resting place, full of summer sounds and scents.Plato, Phaedrus, c. 370 BCE
