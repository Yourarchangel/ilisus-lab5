Ilisus Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Ilisus is operated by Michael Grant Millsap, an individual doing business as Ilisus in Dallas County, Texas ("Ilisus," "we," "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through ilisus.com and our interactive experiences (the "Services"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings in our Terms of Service.

The short version. We collect as little as we can. We never sell personal information. We use analytics to learn which experiences people want, never for advertising. We never use student information for advertising. We do not let other users see anything about you. Our experiences run in your browser and do not send what you do in them to AI systems.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you give us.

Category Examples When
Account information Name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form by our account provider), whether you are an individual or an Educator, and optionally your school name and the grades you teach When you create an Account
Purchase information Billing name, billing address, the last four digits and brand of your card, and purchase history. Full card numbers go directly to Stripe and never reach us When you buy a License
Early-access and newsletter signups Email address and the experiences you said you were interested in When you join a waitlist or mailing list
Feedback and ideas Whatever you choose to write, and your email if you want a reply When you use a feedback or "Give Ideas" form
Support messages Your message and contact details When you email us
Institutional orders The name, title, school and contact details of the person placing the order, and purchase order details When an Institution buys

1.2 Information collected automatically.

  • Technical and log data. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages requested and time of request to deliver pages and block attacks.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, and Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages and experiences people view, click and sign up for. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and the pages you view. We configure it for measurement only: Google signals and ad personalization are off, and we do not link analytics data to your name or email. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies.
  • Fonts. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when the font loads.
  • Experience progress. Experiences may save your place, settings and progress in your own browser's local storage. That information stays on your device unless you are signed in and choose to sync it to your Account.
  • Cookies and similar storage. See our Cookie Notice.

1.3 Information we do not collect. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. We do not collect precise geolocation, biometric data, government ID numbers, or Social Security numbers. Students using a class link are not asked for any personal information.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information only to:

  1. provide, operate and secure the Services, including verifying Licenses and enforcing access controls;
  2. measure, in aggregate, how visitors use our website and experiences;
  3. process payments, send receipts, and send account notices, such as receipts and notice of important changes to an Experience you bought;
  4. respond to your messages and provide support;
  5. send early-access invitations and product updates you asked for, which you may unsubscribe from at any time;
  6. understand, in aggregate, which experiences people want, so we can decide what to build;
  7. improve and fix the Services;
  8. detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and
  9. comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do not use personal information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only as follows.

3.1 Service providers. We use the following providers to run Ilisus. They may use personal information only to provide services to us and must protect it.

3.2 Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith it is required by law, subpoena or court order, or needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Ilisus, our users or the public.

3.3 Business transfers. If Ilisus is reorganized into a company owned or controlled by Michael Grant Millsap, or the business is sold or merged, personal information may transfer to the successor. The successor must honor this Privacy Policy for information collected under it, including our commitments about student information.

3.4 With your direction. We share information when you ask us to, for example when an Educator asks us to send an invoice to a school business office.

3.5 De-identified data. We may share aggregate statistics that cannot reasonably identify you, such as the number of teachers using an experience.

5. Security

We maintain a written information security program appropriate to our size and the sensitivity of the information we hold. It includes multi-factor authentication on our administrative accounts, encryption in transit, least-privilege access, vendor review, and an incident response plan. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

6. Your Choices and Rights

6.1 Rights for everyone. Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your personal information and give you a copy in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law; and
  • stop sending you marketing email.

To make a request, email hello@ilisus.com from the address on your Account. We will verify your identity, respond within 45 days, and never charge you or treat you differently for asking. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to the appeal within 60 days. Texas residents who are not satisfied with an appeal may contact the Texas Attorney General.

6.2 Authorized agents. You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity directly.

6.3 Marketing email. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Transactional messages, such as receipts and account notices, will still be sent while you have a paid License.

6.4 Analytics choices. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is no sale to opt out of. To stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits, block cookies for ilisus.com in your browser or install Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

7. Children and Students

The Services are designed for people 13 and older and for classroom use directed by Educators. Our Children's Privacy and Student Data Notice explains how we handle any information about children under 13 and Students, including our obligations under COPPA, FERPA and Texas Education Code Chapter 32. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information without consent, email hello@ilisus.com and we will delete it.

8. Location of Processing

Ilisus is based in the United States, and our providers store information in the United States and other countries where they operate. If you use the Services from outside the United States, you understand your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from yours.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will post any change here with a new Effective Date. If a change is material, we will notify Account holders by email at least 30 days before it takes effect. We will not use personal information in a materially different way than described when it was collected without your consent.
