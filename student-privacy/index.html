Ilisus Children's Privacy and Student Data Notice

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Notice is for parents, guardians, Educators and Institutions. It supplements our Privacy Policy and explains how Ilisus protects children and Students. If this Notice conflicts with the Privacy Policy on a matter involving a child or Student, this Notice controls.

Our commitments, in brief. We do not require Students to give us any personal information. We never sell Student information. We never use it for advertising or to build profiles for non-school purposes. We delete it when a school asks.

1. How Students Use Ilisus

1.1 Class links (the default). An Educator buys a License and shares a class access link or code. Students open the experience in a browser without creating an account and without entering their name, email or any other personal information. Progress is saved only in the Student's own browser. In this model, Ilisus does not ask Students for personal information. Our sites use analytics cookies, described in Section 2.4, to measure how the experiences are used.

1.2 Student accounts (only if offered by agreement). If we later offer Student accounts, we will offer them only to Institutions that agree to them in writing. We would collect only the minimum needed: a Student's first name or nickname, a class code, and progress within experiences. We would not collect email addresses from Students under 13.

1.3 Students 13 and older outside school. Teens 13 to 17 may hold their own Account only with a parent's involvement, as stated in our Terms.

2. Children Under 13 (COPPA Notice)

This section is our notice under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and the Federal Trade Commission's COPPA Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 312.

2.1 Operator. Michael Grant Millsap, doing business as Ilisus, Dallas, Texas. Email: hello@ilisus.com. A parent may also ask us for our mailing address and telephone number at that email.

2.2 What we collect. Through class links, nothing. If a child under 13 sends us personal information anyway, for example in a feedback form, we delete it promptly once we learn of it. Through any school-authorized Student account, only the items in Section 1.2.

2.3 How we use it. Solely to provide the experiences to the Student for the Institution's educational purpose, to maintain and secure the Services, and as the law requires. Never for advertising, marketing or AI training.

2.4 Disclosure and analytics. We do not disclose children's personal information to third parties, except to service providers that host, secure and measure the Services for us, or when the law requires. Our sites use Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which collect persistent identifiers such as cookie IDs and IP addresses. We use them only to measure and improve the experiences, which COPPA treats as support for internal operations. We do not use them to contact a child, to build a profile of a child, or for advertising, and Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. No other third party may collect personal information from children through the Services.

2.5 School authorization. Where an Institution contracts with us for use of the Services solely for the benefit of its Students and for no other commercial purpose, the Institution may consent on behalf of parents to the collection described in Section 1.2, consistent with the FTC's long-standing guidance. The Institution remains responsible for deciding whether to notify parents or obtain their consent directly.

2.6 Parents' rights. A parent may at any time ask to review the personal information we hold about their child, ask us to delete it, and refuse further collection or use. Email hello@ilisus.com. For school accounts, we will usually route the request through the child's Institution, which controls the records, and we will act on its instructions. We will verify that the requester is the child's parent before releasing information.

2.7 Retention and security. We keep children's information only as long as the Institution's educational purpose requires, then delete it. Our written information security program and retention schedule are described in the Privacy Policy.

3. Commitments to Schools (FERPA)

3.1 School official. When an Institution gives us, or directs Students to give us, information from education records, we act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest under 34 C.F.R. § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B). We perform a service the Institution would otherwise use its own staff for, and we are under the Institution's direct control with respect to use and maintenance of those records.

3.2 Ownership. The Institution owns and controls Student records. We use them only for the purposes the Institution authorizes and do not redisclose them except as FERPA permits.

3.3 Access and correction. We will help the Institution respond to parent and eligible-student requests to inspect, review and correct records.

4. Commitments Under State Student Privacy Laws

These commitments satisfy Texas Education Code Chapter 32, Subchapter D, and the similar student privacy laws of other states, including California's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act. Ilisus will not knowingly:

  1. engage in targeted advertising based on any information, including persistent identifiers, acquired through school use of the Services;
  2. use school-use information to create a profile of a Student, except for a school purpose;
  3. sell or rent any Student's information; or
  4. disclose Student information except as those laws allow, such as to service providers bound by contract to use it only to provide services to us.

We will also:

  • maintain reasonable security procedures and practices designed to protect Student information from unauthorized access, deletion, use, modification or disclosure;
  • delete a Student's information within 30 days of a verified request from the Student's Institution, and never later than the 60 days Texas law allows, unless the Student or parent consents to keep it;
  • notify an affected Institution without unreasonable delay, and within 72 hours of confirming a security incident involving its Student information where practicable, so it can meet its own notice duties; and
  • consider in good faith any data privacy agreement an Institution asks us to sign, including the Texas Student Privacy Alliance agreement.

We may use de-identified, aggregate information that cannot reasonably be linked to a Student to improve our experiences and to show that they work, as these laws allow.

5. Content Suitability

Our experiences are built for middle school, high school and adult learners. Some depict war, slavery, violence and mature themes as they occurred in history, and some scripture-based content is gated behind a content note. Educators should preview each experience before assigning it. See our Educational Content, Accuracy and AI Disclosure.

Questions, requests and data privacy agreements: hello@ilisus.com, with the subject line "Student Privacy."
